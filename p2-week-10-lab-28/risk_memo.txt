MEMORANDUM

TO: Executive Leadership / Board of Directors
FROM: C.K. Bachoo, Cloud Security Engineer (TKH IF-CS-26)
DATE: August 12, 2026
SUBJECT: Business Risk — Misconfigured Cloud Storage Access

1. A cloud storage location holding company data was left open with overly broad access permissions, meaning people or systems outside our intended control could reach it.
2. This creates a direct risk of customer data exposure, regulatory fines, and loss of trust if the data is stolen or leaked.
3. We will immediately restrict access to only approved roles, block public exposure, and keep continuous monitoring in place so this cannot recur unnoticed.
